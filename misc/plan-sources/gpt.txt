# 1) Consistency problems in your current mapping

### 🔴 Case inconsistencies

You sometimes normalize to lowercase, sometimes uppercase, sometimes mixed:

* `hms` vs `HMS`
* `PAPAYA` vs `eToro` vs `Wix`
* `NiCE` ← this one is just incorrect branding

👉 Recommendation:
Pick one standard:

* **Option A (best for APIs):** exact official brand casing (`eToro`, `monday.com`, `eBay`)
* **Option B (simplest):** PascalCase (`Papaya`, `Etoro`, `Ebay`)

Right now you're mixing both.

---

### 🔴 Duplicates in mapping

You have duplicates like:

* `KPMGIsrael = KPMG` (twice)

👉 Not harmful, but signals your pipeline isn't deduping inputs.

---

### 🔴 Over-aggressive shortening (possible data loss)

* `MorningByGreen → Morning`
  → ambiguous, could collide with other "Morning"

* `StraussWater → Strauss`
  → different division, might matter

# 3) Smart improvements (this is where you level up)

## 🧠 A. Add rule-based normalization before manual mapping

Instead of hardcoding everything, add preprocessing:

### Normalize:

* remove `Israel`, `Ltd`, `Inc`, `Group`, `Technologies`
* remove domains: `.com`, `.co.il`

```ts

  .replace(/(Israel|Ltd|Inc|Group|Technologies)/gi, '')
```
